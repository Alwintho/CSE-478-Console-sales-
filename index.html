The takeaway

A winning console and a leading company are different things. Looking at the whole tracked portfolio, including overlapping generations, helps explain that difference.

Behind the story

The records behind the charts.

One measurement throughout

All sales figures come from VGChartz’s global yearly hardware comparison. They are estimated units sold to consumers. We do not combine them with manufacturer shipment totals.

VGChartz revises estimates. This page uses a saved source snapshot so its figures can be reproduced.

Read the VGChartz methodology

What the data can tell us

The dataset includes 14 console families and 123 reported console-year records from 2006 through 2025. It excludes the incomplete 2026 year. Company and hardware classifications are added metadata.

Blank or absent observations stay missing. A console’s first recorded year is not automatically its release date. Totals and shares describe available records, and cannot establish why sales changed.
